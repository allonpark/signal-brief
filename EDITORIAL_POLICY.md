# Signal Brief public editorial policy

Applies only to this repository and Signal Brief. Effective 2026-10-27.

- Trace technology → adoption and production → value capture → earnings and cash flow → expectations embedded in price → conditional investment judgment.
- Cover technology broadly, using semiconductors as an analytical foundation: AI infrastructure, power, cooling, optics, nuclear and robotics when evidence is material.
- Prioritize earnings and valuation, then industry supply/demand, price and flows, and technical validation. Technical quality, business success and investment returns are separate claims.
- Publish two or three material developments by default. No fixed five-story or sector quotas; do not fill space when the judgment is unchanged.
- Distinguish technical watchlists from investment candidates and state the evidence needed for promotion. Prefer orders, production, revenue and cash conversion over unverified claims.
- Compare named Korea-listed ETFs using actual holdings, overlap, concentration, costs, liquidity and currency exposure. State data dates. Missing current weights or valuation limits the conclusion; do not invent values or entry levels.
- Compare covered-call products by reinvested total return, upside participation, downside and cost rather than distribution yield alone.
- Separate verified facts, management guidance and analytical inference. Use primary filings, IR, issuer holdings, official releases and papers. Provide publication dates, event dates and falsification conditions.
- Include Korean text with English titles and summaries; core analytical sections should remain useful in both languages.
- Public output contains public-source technology, company and ETF analysis and general conditional scenarios only. Never include private holdings, amounts, P/L, account information, screenshots or personal execution plans in source, commits, issues or logs.
- Preserve card navigation, story detail links, language switching and archives. Append dated editions without rewriting historical editions. Validate JSON, sources, illustrations and links before release, then verify the exact commit's GitHub Pages deployment.
- This update does not create or change an automated publishing schedule.

## Edition presentation

- Each story needs a distinct content-specific illustration that conveys its mechanism or decision boundary. Label conceptual diagrams and distinguish reported data from scheduled events.
- The top summary states that edition's actual findings in one or two concise sentences, followed by explicit judgments. Avoid generic slogans.
- Group all stories from one edition in an in-page reading deck with an overview, contents, previous/next controls, keyboard navigation and an optional expanded view. Preserve original deep links and language switching.
